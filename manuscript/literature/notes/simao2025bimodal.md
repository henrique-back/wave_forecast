**Simão, M.L., Videiro, P.M., Sudati Sagrilo, L.V., de Oliveira, M.C. (2025).** Proceedings of

## Summary
Investigates how bimodal (combined swell and wind-sea) sea states affect the extreme response
of offshore mooring systems on the Brazilian continental margin — a region where swell arriving
from distant generation areas routinely coexists with locally generated wind sea, producing
two-peaked spectra. Authors are at COPPE/UFRJ and Petrobras.
