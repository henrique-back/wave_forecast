# A Deep Learning-Based Approach for Empirical Modeling of Single-Point Wave Spectra in Open Oceans

**Song, Y., Jiang, H. (2023).** *Journal of Physical Oceanography*, 53(9). DOI:
10.1175/JPO-D-22-0198.1. (Exact page range not independently confirmed beyond the article's
opening pages — check the publisher record before quoting a page number.)

## Summary
A CNN trained on ERA5 reanalysis wind fields (a polar grid around a target point, past-10-day
history) predicts directional wave spectra, with ERA5/WAVEWATCH-III-consistent spectra (not buoy
observations) as the training target. Validated globally.

## Relevance to this manuscript
High — directly comparable as a deep-learning-for-directional-spectra problem, but the inputs
are remote wind fields and the target is model-simulated (not observed buoy) spectra, unlike
this project's single-buoy, spectrum-autoregressive setup.
