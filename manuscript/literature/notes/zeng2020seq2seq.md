# A Sequence-to-Sequence Model Based on Attention Mechanism for Wave Spectrum Prediction

**Zeng, X., Qi, L., Yi, T., Liu, T. (2020).** *2020 11th International Conference on Awareness
Science and Technology (iCAST)*, Qingdao, China. DOI: 10.1109/iCAST51195.2020.9319473.

## Summary
A Bi-GRU encoder-decoder with attention predicts wave spectral density from simulated
acceleration time series (WAFO-generated Torsethaugen spectra, 2800 samples), beating COV/PMEM
/periodogram baselines on MAE/RMSE.

## Relevance to this manuscript
High — the closest prior-art match among the local corpus for "attention-based deep learning
directly targeting wave spectral density prediction," though it estimates from acceleration
(not multi-step temporal forecasting from spectral history) and uses synthetic data only.

## Suggested use
Introduction's prior-ML-for-wave-spectra survey and old-vs-new contrast paragraph — a legitimate
predecessor to cite before introducing the frequency-structured transformer embedding as an
advance over generic sequence-to-sequence attention.
