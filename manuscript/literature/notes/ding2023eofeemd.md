# Regional Forecasting of Significant Wave Height and Mean Wave Period Using EOF-EEMD-SCINet Hybrid Model

**Ding, J., Deng, F., Liu, Q., Wang, J. (2023).** *Applied Ocean Research*, 136, 103582.
DOI: 10.1016/j.apor.2023.103582.

## Summary
Combines EOF (spatial dimensionality reduction), EEMD (signal decomposition), and SCINet (a
tree-structured CNN forecaster) to jointly predict regional Hs and mean wave period from ERA5
reanalysis over the South China Sea, at 24/48/72h leads.

## Relevance to this manuscript
Medium — ML wave forecasting, but gridded/regional rather than single-buoy, and a
decomposition+CNN family rather than spectra/transformer/directional content.

## Suggested use
Prior hybrid-ML Hs/period forecasting baseline for contrast in the Introduction's related-work
survey; not a close architectural or target-variable match.
