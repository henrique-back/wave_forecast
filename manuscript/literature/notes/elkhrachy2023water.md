# Novel Ocean Wave Height and Energy Spectrum Forecasting Approaches: An Application of Semi-Analytical and Machine Learning Models

**Elkhrachy, I., Alhamami, A., Alyami, S.H., Alviz-Meza, A. (2023).** *Water*, 15(18), 3254.
DOI: 10.3390/w15183254.

## Summary
Compares an SMB semi-analytical model against Emotional ANN (EANN) and Wavelet ANN (WANN) for
forecasting Hs and energy-spectrum density at Aleutian Basin and Gulf of Mexico buoys, across
hourly/12h/daily scales. EANN performs best for hourly Hs and best spectral-peak accuracy.

## Relevance to this manuscript
High — same task family (ML forecasting of Hs + energy spectrum from buoy data), though a
different architecture (EANN/WANN, not transformer).

## Suggested use
Prior ML baseline for Hs+spectrum forecasting to contrast the transformer approach against; also
supports the "gap" framing (no transformer/attention-based method tested there).
