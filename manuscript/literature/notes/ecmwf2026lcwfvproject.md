# Project — WMO Lead Centre for Wave Forecast Verification (LC-WFV)

**ECMWF (2026).** The Lead Centre's statement of objectives. The author supplied this link on
2026-09-24 as the page `hernandez2025intercomparison` relies on.

## Summary
The page states the Lead Centre's objective as coordinating wave forecast verification. It does
this by giving centres of the Joint Technical Commission for Oceanography and Marine Meteorology
(JCOMM) that produce global or ocean-basin wave forecasts a facility to deposit their gridded
forecast fields and receive the verification statistics computed for them. It also archives those
statistics for performance trends, monitors incoming fields, collects annual system-change reports,
and provides "ftp access to the buoy observations data sets used to perform the standard
verification". It lists 18 potential stakeholder centres: BoM, DMI, DWD, ECCC, ECMWF, FNMOC, JMA,
KMA, LOPS, METEOAM, METNO, METFR, NCEP, NIWA, NZMS, PRTOS, SHN and UKMO.

**It does not list the verified parameters.** That information is on the sibling Parameters page,
`ecmwf2019lcwfvparameters`. It says nothing about spectra.

## Relevance to this manuscript
Medium. It supports the claim that the operational intercomparison verifies forecasts against buoy
observations, and it corroborates the scale of the effort (18 centres).
