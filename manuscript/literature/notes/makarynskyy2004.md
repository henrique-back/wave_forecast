# Improving Wave Predictions with Artificial Neural Networks

**Makarynskyy, O. (2004).** *Ocean Engineering*, 31(5-6), 709-724. DOI: 10.1016/j.oceaneng.2003.05.003.

## Summary
Uses ANNs (resilient backpropagation) to correct/improve 1-24h-lead numerical wave forecasts of
Hs and zero-crossing period at two Irish buoys (M1, M2), comparing a pure-correction approach
against one that merges prior measurements with the numerical forecast. Both ML-augmented
approaches improve on the raw numerical forecast.

## Relevance to this manuscript
High — an early ML (feedforward ANN) Hs/period forecasting precedent from buoy data, at lead
times comparable to (a subset of) this project's own 12/24/48h grid.

## Suggested use
Prior ML-based Hs-forecasting baseline for the Introduction's old-vs-new method contrast: an
early, feedforward, scalar-output precedent, motivating why a sequence-aware transformer
operating on the full spectrum is a meaningful step beyond forecast-correction ANNs.
