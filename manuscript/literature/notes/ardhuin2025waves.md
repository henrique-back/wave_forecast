# Ocean Waves in Geosciences, Parts 1 and 2: General Wave Topics from Deep to Shallow Water

**Ardhuin, Fabrice (2025).** Course notes, Laboratoire d'Océanographie Physique et Spatiale,
Brest. DOI: 10.13140/RG.2.2.16019.78888/12. Dated 2025-10-31 on the document's own title page
(confirmed directly by re-reading page 1 of the local PDF, 2026-09-18) — this is a
continuously-updated ResearchGate technical report/teaching document, **not** a peer-reviewed
journal article. Companion GitHub repo: https://github.com/ardhuin/waves_in_geosciences.

## Summary
A comprehensive wave-theory reference/course text: linear wave theory, frequency/directional
spectra estimation from buoys and other sensors, spectral parameters (Hs, Tp), and wave
evolution physics.

## Relevance to this manuscript
Medium — a foundational physics reference, not ML-specific, but directly useful for
definitional/background citations.

## Suggested use
Usable as-is for general background, but for the *specific* facts this manuscript needs
(spectral moments $m_n$, $H_s = 4\sqrt{m_0}$, and the buoy-derived directional parameters
alpha1/alpha2/r1/r2), prefer the more standard peer-reviewed citations found during the same
literature pass instead: **Kuik et al. (1988)** for the buoy-processing method itself,
**Longuet-Higgins et al. (1963)** for its historical origin, **NDBC Technical Document 03-01**
(Earle, 2003) for NDBC's own operational formulas, and **Holthuijsen (2007)** for the general
spectral-moments/Hs textbook treatment (all four added to `refs.bib` as `kuik1988`,
`longuethiggins1963`, `ndbc2003techdoc`, `holthuijsen2007`). Use Ardhuin only for material those
four don't cover.
