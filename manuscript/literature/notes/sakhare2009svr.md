# Derivation of Wave Spectrum Using Data Driven Methods

**Sakhare, S., Deo, M.C. (2009).** *Marine Structures*, 22, 594-609.
DOI: 10.1016/j.marstruc.2008.12.004.

## Summary
Uses Support Vector Regression and Model Trees to derive full spectral density curves from just
Hs and Tz at two NDBC/NIOT buoy sites, showing both outperform parametric PM/JONSWAP fits.

## Relevance to this manuscript
Medium — classical (non-deep) ML deriving full spectral shape from scalar bulk parameters, on
buoy data (same NDBC data family used here), conceptually adjacent to the magnitude/shape
decoupling idea but in the reverse direction (bulk params -> shape, via regression, not a
learned shape+magnitude recombination) and not a forecasting task.

## Suggested use
Relevant precedent for the general idea that spectral shape can be decoupled from magnitude
parameters — a motivating citation for the shape/magnitude split in the Introduction or
Discussion, noting the direction and classical-ML method differ from this project's approach.
