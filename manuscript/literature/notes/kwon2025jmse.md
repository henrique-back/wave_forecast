# Parametric Estimation of Directional Wave Spectra from Moored FPSO Motion Data Using Optimized Artificial Neural Networks

**Kwon, D.-S., Kim, S.-J., Jin, C., Kim, M. (2025).** *Journal of Marine Science and
Engineering*, 13(1), 69. DOI: 10.3390/jmse13010069.

## Summary
Uses ANNs (compared against SVM, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, Ridge Regression) to
inversely estimate parametric directional wave spectrum parameters (Hs, Tp, mean direction,
JONSWAP enhancement factor, spreading factor) from simulated FPSO vessel motion data (OrcaFlex
digital twin), not from buoy spectral measurements.

## Relevance to this manuscript
Low — same broad domain (directional wave spectra, ML) but solves an inverse sensing problem
(vessel-motion-to-spectrum-parameters) rather than spectral forecasting; no temporal forecasting
and no buoy-spectrum data.

## Suggested use
No direct claim support identified; at most a tangential example of ML applied to
directional-spectrum parameter estimation. Compare/contrast with `kwon2026transformer` (same
first author, later paper, transformer-based) if discussing this author group's line of work.
