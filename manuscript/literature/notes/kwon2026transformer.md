# Transformers and Neural Networks for Estimation of Parameters of Multi-Directional Waves from Rich Statistics of FPSO Motion Signals

**Kwon, D.-S., Jin, C., Kim, M., Kim, S.-J. (2026).** *Applied Ocean Research*, 166, 104927.
DOI: 10.1016/j.apor.2026.104927.

## Summary
Trains an ANN and a transformer-based ensemble on up to 122 statistical features of simulated
FPSO 6DOF motions (from OrcaFlex under ERA5-derived met-ocean conditions) to inversely estimate
5 parametric directional-spectrum parameters (Hs, Tp, mean direction, JONSWAP gamma, spreading
factor s). The transformer ensemble is more robust across sea states than the ANN.

## Relevance to this manuscript
High — directly comparable in that a transformer is applied to directional wave-spectrum
parameters (spreading/direction, analogous to this project's alpha1/alpha2/r1/r2 channels),
though the task is inverse estimation from vessel motion, not temporal forecasting from a
buoy's own spectral history.

## Suggested use
Strong candidate citation establishing that transformers are already being applied to
directional wave-spectrum parameter estimation; useful for the Introduction's gap statement,
distinguishing this project by input modality (buoy spectral history, not vessel motion) and
task framing (forecasting, not inversion).
