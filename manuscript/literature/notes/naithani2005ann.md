# Estimation of Wave Spectral Shapes Using ANN

**Naithani, R., Deo, M.C. (2005).** *Advances in Engineering Software*, 36, 750-756.
DOI: 10.1016/j.advengsoft.2005.03.021.

## Summary
A feedforward ANN trained to output the full spectral density shape (28 frequency bins) from
four bulk parameters (Hs, Tz, spectral width, peakedness), using NDBC (Cape Hatteras) and Indian
coastal buoy data. Shown to outperform standard parametric shapes (PM/JONSWAP/Scott's).

## Relevance to this manuscript
High — a direct precedent for decoupling spectral shape from bulk magnitude parameters via a
neural network, i.e. the inverse direction of this project's shape/magnitude split (here:
params -> shape; this project: two learned forecasters, shape and magnitude, recombined).

## Suggested use
Motivating/justifying citation for the shape/magnitude decomposition in Methods — an early,
citable precedent that neural nets can represent spectral shape independently of scalar wave
parameters better than parametric spectra, even though the direction and forecasting-vs
-estimation framing differ.
