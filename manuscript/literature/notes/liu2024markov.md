# A Markov Prediction Model for Sea-Level Fluctuations Based on Wave Spectrum

**Liu, F., Lai, X., Wen, H., Lu, C., Wang, Y., Cao, W., Wu, M. (2024).**
*Proceedings of the 43rd Chinese Control Conference*, Kunming, China, pp. 3547-3551.

## Summary
A purely simulated study: a JONSWAP spectrum is used to synthesize sea-surface elevation, then a
first-order Markov chain (state-transition matrix over wave-height bins) predicts sea-level over
a 5-second window. No real buoy data.

## Relevance to this manuscript
Low — different method family (Markov chain, not ML/DL), different timescale (seconds, not
hours), synthetic data only; the only commonality is JONSWAP-spectrum usage.

## Suggested use
At most a generic JONSWAP-spectrum citation; not useful for a forecasting-method comparison.
Unlikely to be cited.
