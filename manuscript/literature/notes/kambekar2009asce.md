# Data Driven Methods to Analyze Wave Buoy Observations

**Kambekar, A.R., Deo, M.C., Daga, M., Sakhare, S. (2009).** *Computing in Civil Engineering
(2009)*, Proceedings of the 2009 ASCE International Workshop on Computing in Civil Engineering,
Austin, TX, pp. 398-409. DOI: 10.1061/41052(346)40.

**Note:** the locally-held PDF itself shows pages 398-401; the Crossref/ASCE Library record
consistently gives 398-409. Worth a quick manual check against the physical proceedings volume
if the exact page range matters (e.g. for a page-specific quote).

## Summary
Applies genetic programming, model trees, SVR, and locally-weighted regression to hindcast Hs
/Tz from wind speed/direction at two Indian buoys (1998-2006); a review-style paper on non-ANN
data-driven methods, not on full spectra.

## Relevance to this manuscript
Medium — same domain (data-driven buoy wave-parameter estimation) but classical ML, and the
target is Hs/Tz, not spectra.

## Suggested use
Could support a broader "data-driven wave modelling" background sentence in the Introduction;
not a strong methodological comparator.
